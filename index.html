// About

Biography

I'm an experimental fluid dynamicist (Ph.D. expected January 2027) specializing in the development and application of advanced optical diagnostics for turbulent flows. My dissertation — "Geometry-Induced Three-Dimensional Separation of Turbulent Boundary Layers over Directional Riblet Surfaces" — uses directional riblet textures to impose high- and low-momentum pathways in the near-wall flow, showing that small surface modifications can drive outsized changes in separation behavior.

With 5+ years designing and executing multi-campaign experiments in air and water facilities at high Reynolds numbers, my toolkit spans planar, high-speed (1 kHz), and stereoscopic PIV; volumetric Shake-the-Box Lagrangian particle tracking (7.3 kHz); and simultaneous PIV/PLIF of scalar mixing in particle-laden turbulent jets — delivering validation-grade datasets that anchor computational and reduced-order models.

// Selected Work

Research

Dissertation core

Geometry-induced 3D separation over directional riblets

Converging–diverging riblets over a backward-facing ramp create spanwise-periodic high- and low-momentum pathways. Multi-campaign planar and stereoscopic PIV shows separation-region turbulence differs by up to 30% between pathways — delayed separation and earlier reattachment in HMPs — while bulk boundary-layer properties change by only a few percent.

PIVSeparation controlRiblets

Multiphase flows

Scalar mixing in particle-laden turbulent jets

Simultaneous PIV/PLIF on particle-laden axisymmetric jets in water: how highly inertial particles (tungsten carbide, 200 µm, 1–5% mass loading) modulate scalar concentration fields and dissipation in high-Schmidt-number, variable-density mixing.

High-speed & volumetric diagnostics at scale

Time-resolved planar PIV (1 kHz) with time-lag correlations tying upstream boundary-layer content to separation dynamics; volumetric STB Lagrangian tracking at 7.3 kHz across overlapping volumes — 10,000+ fields per condition with automated pipelines cutting analysis turnaround 10×.
